How the rule types differ
Exact subreddits
Hide every feed post from that community. Case doesn't matter, and r/Fauxmoi, /r/Fauxmoi and Fauxmoi are the same. This is the most precise rule type.
Wildcard patterns
Match subreddit names. * means “any characters” and ? means “one character”: *snark* matches any name containing “snark”, News* names starting with “News”. They're powerful but broad, so check them with the rule tester and use exclusions to carve out false positives.
Title keywords
Match whole words or phrases in post titles, in any subreddit. art won't match “party”. Add a trailing * to match word beginnings (comic* matches “comics”).
Exclusions
Subreddits (or patterns) this category should never hide, even if one of its rules matches. The global allowlist below overrides every category.

Rules are checked in this order: allowlist, then exact subreddits, then patterns, then keywords. The first match wins. Single-subreddit pages and comment pages are never filtered, so you can always visit a blocked community directly.

    Rule tester

    Export saves all categories, rules and settings as JSON. Import replaces your current settings after you confirm.
