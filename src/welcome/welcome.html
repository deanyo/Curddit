Feed Curator for Reddit

Hide the communities and topics you don't want in your Reddit feeds.

What would you like to hide?

Nothing is filtered until you choose. Changes save instantly, and you can change them any time from the toolbar button.

    Where should it filter?

    Good to know

    • Hover any post on Reddit and click the small ⊘ button to hide its community, add it to a category, or always show it.
    • Visiting a community directly (for example r/politics) is never filtered.
    • Everything stays in your browser. No account, no tracking, no network requests.
