# Privacy policy: Feed Curator for Reddit

*Last updated: 26 September 2026*

Feed Curator for Reddit collects **no data**.

- **What it stores:** your categories, rules and settings, plus counts of how many posts it has hidden. These are stored only in your browser's extension storage (`storage.local` and `storage.session`) and never leave your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.
- **What it reads:** on reddit.com pages only, it reads each feed post's subreddit name, title and post ID from the page, so it can decide locally whether to hide that post. This information is not stored, apart from recently seen post IDs kept in memory and session storage to avoid double-counting. It is never transmitted.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or remote code.
- **Your Reddit account:** it does not use your Reddit login, the Reddit API, or your account settings, and never changes them.
- **Sharing:** nothing is collected, so nothing is sold, shared or transferred.

The source code is public: https://github.com/deanyo/Curddit. Questions: https://github.com/deanyo/Curddit/issues
